02
Lend

Supply MSTR to a lending market. Borrowers post it as collateral or borrow it; you earn the supply rate.

Risk: the rate can be 0% when nobody borrows, and a fast crash can leave bad debt in the market.

Borrow

Keep your MSTR and borrow stablecoins against it.

Risk: if MSTR falls past the liquidation level, your collateral is sold. Fixed-term loans still have to be repaid on time.
