How it works

Three things, then you’re caught up.

01

Where tokens come from

A market maker buys real MSTR shares, the issuer locks them and mints the same number of tokens. To exit, tokens are burned and the shares released. You just buy and sell.

02

It trades 24/7. Nasdaq doesn’t.

MSTR tokens trade nights and weekends, so the token price can drift away from the last share price. Market makers pull it back by minting and burning when they can.

03

Good to know

Tokens give price exposure, not shareholder votes. Check Robinhood’s terms for who can hold them.
