How the pool works
01

$STRATEGY trades against real tokenized MSTR in one Uniswap pool.

02

Every buy adds MSTR to the pool. Every sell takes some out.

03

Part of every trade’s fee is added back to the pool, permanently. That’s the floor: it only rises.

The liquidity pool is locked and can’t be withdrawn. Pool MSTR is the pool’s inventory, not a redemption right.

How we calculate each number
MSTR in the pool
Tokenized MSTR held by the $STRATEGY/MSTR pool on Robinhood Chain. It is the pool’s inventory, not a redemption right.
Compounded by fees
MSTR in the pool above what the launch liquidity alone would hold at today’s price. The pool launched with only $STRATEGY, so MSTR on the launch curve came from buyers and MSTR above it came from fees.
MSTR per 1M $STRATEGY
MSTR in the pool ÷ 1B coins × 1M. The coin’s version of Strategy₿ Inc.’s bitcoin per share.
MSTR Yield
MSTR compounded by fees over 30 days ÷ MSTR in the pool 30 days ago. MSTR that came in because the price went up is not counted.
History
One snapshot per day. Until enough days are stored, the chart and yield use placeholder data marked SAMPLE.
