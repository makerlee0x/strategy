How it works.

How tokenized MSTR works, and how the STRATEGY pool works.

1 · Where tokens come from
01
Buy share
A market maker buys real MSTR shares at a broker.
02
Mint
The issuer locks the shares and creates the same number of tokens.
03
Trade
Tokens trade 24/7 on-chain — anyone can buy from the market.
04
Burn
To exit, the market maker returns tokens; they are destroyed and the shares released.

Only approved market makers ("authorized participants") mint and burn. You simply buy and sell. Robinhood mints Mon 02:00 – Sat 02:00 CET; Ondo offers 24/7 mint/redeem on top tokens; xStocks, Dinari and Coinbase use similar KYC-gated desks.

2 · Why the price stays close to MSTR
Token too expensive? Market makers mint more and sell. Too cheap? They buy and burn. Either way, the gap closes.

3 · What happens with dividends

MSTR common pays no dividend today. If it ever did, Robinhood tokens would not send cash: a number in the token contract, the multiplier, goes up — so each token represents slightly more than one share. 5 · How the STRATEGY metrics work
MSTR holdingsMSTR in the STRATEGY/MSTR pool on Robinhood Chain, plus the Strategy Coin Reserve once it exists. Pool MSTR is the pool’s inventory, not a redemption right.
MSTR per 1M STRATEGYMSTR holdings ÷ 1B coins × 1M. The coin’s version of Strategy’s BTC per share.
STRATEGY mNAVSTRATEGY market cap ÷ (MSTR holdings × MSTR price). Strategy Coin’s own mNAV, not Strategy Inc’s. Above 1× means the market values the coin above the MSTR behind it.
MSTR YieldMSTR added by trading fees over a period ÷ MSTR holdings at the start. Fee MSTR is pool MSTR above what the launch curve alone would hold at today’s price: the pool starts single-sided, so MSTR on the curve came from buyers and MSTR above it came from fees. MSTR that came in because the price went up is not counted. Shown for 30 days and year-to-date.
Supply1,000,000,000 STRATEGY, fixed at launch.
