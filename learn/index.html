How it works.

Three ideas. Two minutes.

1 · Where tokens come from
01
Buy share
A market maker buys real MSTR shares at a broker.
02
Mint
The issuer locks the shares and creates the same number of tokens.
03
Trade
Tokens trade 24/7 on-chain — anyone can buy from the market.
04
Burn
To exit, the market maker returns tokens; they are destroyed and the shares released.

Only approved market makers ("authorized participants") mint and burn. You simply buy and sell. Robinhood mints Mon 02:00 – Sat 02:00 CET; Ondo offers 24/7 mint/redeem on top tokens; xStocks, Dinari and Coinbase use similar KYC-gated desks.

2 · Why the price stays close to MSTR
Token too expensive? Market makers mint more and sell. Too cheap? They buy and burn. Either way, the gap closes.

3 · What happens with dividends

MSTR common pays no dividend today. If it ever did, Robinhood tokens would not send cash: a number in the token contract, the multiplier, goes up — so each token represents slightly more than one share.
